# Industry demonstration imagery

These files are illustrative stills generated for Aethelios Atelier’s barber-and-salon demonstration.

They are **not** photographs of Fix It Shop, Katie Guidry, her clients, or a delivered location.

Visual references used for art direction only (not copied):

- https://www.hershesons.com/pages/stores
- https://www.murdocklondon.com/pages/the-murdock-experience

Hands, shears, mirrors, and furnishings were visually checked before use. These stills remain illustrative.

Katie’s completed work is presented separately from the attached cinematic capture of https://fixitshop-app.vercel.app/.
